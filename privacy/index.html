Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how Bang Pa-in Dental Clinic ("we", "the clinic") handles information on this website, including the TikTok Publisher tool at /tiktok.

1. Appointment requests

When you request an appointment, we collect the details you enter (such as your name, phone number, preferred date and time, and the service you are interested in). We use them only to contact you and manage your appointment.

2. TikTok Publisher

The TikTok Publisher is used by the clinic to post its own videos to the clinic's TikTok account. When you connect a TikTok account, we receive from TikTok:

  • Basic profile information (user.info.basic): your TikTok display name, avatar and an account identifier, used to show which account is connected.
  • An access token that allows the app to publish videos you choose (video.publish, video.upload).

The access token is stored only in an encrypted, HTTP-only cookie in your browser for the length of the TikTok session. We do not keep it in a database. Videos and captions you choose are sent directly to TikTok when you confirm a post; we do not keep copies of them. We do not sell or share this information with anyone else.

You can disconnect at any time with the "Disconnect" button, which revokes the app's access, or from your TikTok app settings.

3. Cookies

We use only the cookies needed for the TikTok connection (login state and session). They are not used for advertising.

4. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting the clinic below.
